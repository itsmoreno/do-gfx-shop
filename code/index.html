About

Two people, one obsession with good-looking avatars.

We started D & O Shop because we got tired of seeing great avatars stuck behind flat in-game screenshots. Dan handles the rendering and lighting. Ori handles the poses, composition, and that final layer of polish that makes a render feel intentional instead of generic.

Every order is built from scratch around your avatar. You tell us the vibe - profile picture, group banner, YouTube thumbnail, birthday gift for a friend - and we deliver something you'll actually want to use.
